| **Use free attractions** – Dubai Fountain, Jumeirah Beach, Al Seef historic promenade. | No entry fees. |
| **Pre‑book attraction tickets online** – you get a discount and skip queues. | E‑tickets are usually 5‑15 % cheaper. |
| **Carry a multi‑currency card (e.g., Revolut, Niyo Global)** | Lower conversion fees than cash exchange. |
